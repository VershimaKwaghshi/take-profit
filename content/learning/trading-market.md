# What Is Trading?

Trading is the act of buying and selling financial assets in the hope of making a profit from changes in price.

People trade many different types of assets every day, including:
- Stocks
- Currencies (Forex)
- Cryptocurrencies
- Commodities such as Gold and Oil
- Indices
- Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs)

Although these markets look different, they all work on the same fundamental principle: buyers and sellers agree on a price to exchange an asset.

## Why Do Prices Move?

Prices move because supply and demand are constantly changing.

When more people want to buy an asset than sell it, the price generally rises.

When more people want to sell than buy, the price generally falls.

News, economic reports, company performance, world events, and trader expectations can all influence these decisions.

The market is constantly adjusting as millions of participants buy and sell around the world.

---

## Financial Markets Never Stand Still

Every second, thousands of traders, investors, businesses, banks, and institutions place buy and sell orders.

These orders create continuous price movement across global financial markets.

Whether someone is trading Bitcoin, Apple shares, Gold, or the EUR/USD currency pair, the same basic process is taking place.

---

## Every Market Follows The Same Principle

Regardless of what is being traded, every transaction depends on one simple rule:

Someone buys.  
Someone sells.

A trade only happens when both sides agree on a price.

Understanding this idea is the foundation of everything else you will learn.

The next module explains exactly who those buyers and sellers are and how every trade has two sides.

## Key Takeaway

Trading is simply the exchange of financial assets between buyers and sellers.

Different markets offer different assets, but they all operate using the same core principle of matching buyers and sellers at an agreed price.

Trading itself is not the problem.

The challenge many traders face comes from managing losses, maintaining consistency, and recovering after setbacks.
