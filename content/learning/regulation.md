# Regulation

Financial markets operate under rules designed to promote fairness transparency and confidence

Many brokers are supervised by financial regulators that establish standards for how they conduct their business

These rules help create a safer environment for market participants

![Regulation](/learning/regulation.svg)

---

## What Is A Financial Regulator

A financial regulator is an organisation that oversees financial firms operating within its jurisdiction

Regulators set standards monitor compliance and may take action when firms fail to meet their obligations

Different countries have different regulators and each regulator has authority only within its own jurisdiction

Examples include
* Securities and Exchange Commission in Nigeria
* Financial Conduct Authority in the United Kingdom
* Commodity Futures Trading Commission and National Futures Association in the United States
* Australian Securities and Investments Commission in Australia

---

## What Do Regulators Do

Financial regulators establish rules that brokers are expected to follow

These rules may include
* Maintaining sufficient operating capital
* Protecting client funds where required by local regulations
* Providing fair and transparent information
* Managing operational and financial risks
* Following reporting and compliance requirements

The exact requirements depend on the country and the regulator

---

## Why Regulation Matters

Regulation helps increase trust in financial markets

A regulated broker is generally expected to meet standards set by its regulator and remain subject to ongoing oversight

However regulation does not eliminate trading risk or guarantee that a trader will make a profit

Traders should always understand the risks involved before participating in financial markets

---

## Choosing A Broker

Before opening an account it is good practice to check
* Which regulator oversees the broker
* The country where the broker is licensed
* The products and services the broker is authorised to offer
* Whether the broker meets your trading needs

Understanding who regulates a broker is one part of making an informed decision

---

## Key Takeaway

Financial regulators help oversee brokers and establish rules that support fair and transparent financial markets

Their role is to promote market integrity and supervise regulated firms within their jurisdiction
