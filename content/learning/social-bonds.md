# Social Bond

## What Is A Social Bond

Life does not stop because you are trading.

You may have an emergency, want to start a new project, expand a business, pay school fees, or buy a home.

Many traders solve this by withdrawing money from their trading account or closing profitable positions.

The Social Bond provides another option.

Instead of interrupting your trading, you can request liquidity while your trading account continues operating.

![Social Bond](/learning/social-bond.svg)

---

## Why Does The Social Bond Exist

A trading account is built to generate long term value.

Withdrawing from it whenever money is needed can reduce its growth and interrupt trading.

The Social Bond allows traders to access liquidity without giving up the opportunities already created inside their trading account.

This allows traders to meet financial needs while their trading continues.

---

## How It Works

You simply choose the amount you need and submit a Social Bond request.

The requested amount immediately becomes a lien against your trading account for the selected repayment period.

The lien does not stop you from trading.

It simply protects the amount while the Social Bond remains active.

Once approved, the money is released and can be used for any purpose.

---

## Who Provides The Liquidity

Social Bonds are funded by facilitators.

A facilitator may be another trader, a financial institution, a fintech company, a licensed credit union, or Take Profit.

They provide the capital that allows approved requests to be completed.

Traders who prefer stable returns instead of market exposure can also participate by becoming facilitators.

---

## How Bonders Participate

Every Social Bond is supported by bonders.

When a request is created, the platform selects traders with suitable trading accounts to support that request.

Each bonder carries only a small portion of the total responsibility.

Nothing is deducted from them when the request is approved.

Their responsibility only becomes active if recovery cannot be completed through the normal platform recovery process.

---

## The Activation Fee

When a Social Bond is withdrawn, a twenty percent activation fee is deducted.

Half of this fee is shared among the participating bonders.

Thirty percent goes to the facilitator.

The remaining twenty percent belongs to Take Profit.

This allows everyone who supports the Social Bond market to be rewarded for providing liquidity.

---

## Repayment

Every Social Bond has an expiration period.

If repayment is completed before the expiration date, the lien is removed and the Social Bond closes.

The trading account continues operating normally throughout the entire process.

---

## If Repayment Is Delayed

If repayment has not been completed by the expiration date, Take Profit first attempts recovery using its internal recovery system.

Only after those recovery methods have been exhausted do the participating bonders become responsible for their individual portions.

If recovery is completed later, every participating bonder receives their contribution back.

---

## Why Traders Use Social Bonds

The Social Bond allows traders to solve financial needs without interrupting their trading activities.

Instead of closing positions or withdrawing trading capital, they can keep their trading account active while accessing the liquidity they need.

This allows both their financial plans and their trading activities to continue together.

---

## A Simple Example

Imagine Michael has a one hundred dollar trading account.

He needs one hundred dollars to buy equipment for a new business.

Instead of withdrawing from his trading account, he requests a Social Bond.

The request is approved and he receives the money.

His trading account continues operating while the equipment helps him build another source of income.

If his trading account generates profits during the repayment period, those profits can help him settle the Social Bond while his trading account remains active.

Instead of choosing between trading and his new project, he is able to continue with both.

---

## Key Takeaway

The Social Bond allows traders to access liquidity without interrupting their trading activities.

Facilitators provide the capital, bonders support the market, and Take Profit manages the recovery process.

This creates a structured liquidity market where traders can continue building their financial goals while keeping their trading accounts active.
