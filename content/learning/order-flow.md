# Order Flow

When you press Buy or Sell on your trading platform the trade does not happen instantly

Instead your order begins a journey through several systems before it is executed

Understanding this process helps explain how modern financial markets operate

![Order Flow](/learning/order-flow.svg)

---

## Step One

You place a Buy or Sell order using your trading platform

The order contains important details such as the asset you want to trade the trade size and the type of order you are placing

The platform immediately sends this information to your broker

---

## Step Two

The broker receives your order

The broker checks that your trading account is able to place the trade

For example the broker may verify that your account has enough available funds or margin and that the order meets the account requirements

If the order passes these checks it is prepared for execution

---

## Step Three

The broker routes the order

Depending on the broker and the market the order may be sent to a liquidity provider an exchange or another trading venue

The goal is to find available liquidity so the trade can be executed

---

## Step Four

The order is matched

When another market participant is willing to take the opposite side of your trade the order is executed

The execution price depends on the available market prices at that moment

---

## Step Five

The trade is confirmed

After execution the trading platform updates your account

Your position becomes visible and any profit or loss will change as market prices move

---

## Why This Process Matters

Most of this happens in a fraction of a second

Although the process is extremely fast several systems work together behind the scenes to complete every trade

Understanding this journey helps traders see that pressing Buy or Sell starts a process rather than creating a trade instantly

---

## Key Takeaway

Every trade follows a sequence from order placement to execution and confirmation

Your broker receives the order checks that it can be processed routes it to the appropriate market and confirms the completed trade
