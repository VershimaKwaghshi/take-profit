# Proprietary Trading Firms

Many skilled traders have the knowledge to trade successfully but do not have enough personal capital to pursue larger opportunities

Proprietary trading firms often called prop firms were created to help address this challenge

Rather than lending money directly many prop firms evaluate traders before giving them access to larger trading accounts

![Proprietary Trading Firm](/learning/prop-firm.svg)

---

## What Is A Prop Firm

A proprietary trading firm is a company that provides traders with access to trading capital after they meet specific performance requirements

Instead of trading only with their own money successful traders may qualify to trade with capital provided by the firm

In return the trader and the firm usually share a portion of the profits

---

## How The Process Works

Most prop firms follow a structured process

The trader begins with an evaluation

During this stage the trader must demonstrate consistent performance while following defined risk rules

If the trader successfully completes the evaluation the firm may offer access to a funded account

The trader can then continue trading under the firms conditions while sharing a percentage of any eligible profits

---

## Why Evaluations Exist

Evaluations are designed to measure consistency discipline and risk management

They help firms understand how a trader performs before allocating larger amounts of capital

The specific rules and requirements vary from one firm to another

---

## Understanding Funded Accounts

Receiving a funded account does not mean the trader has been given unrestricted ownership of the capital

The trader is granted permission to trade under the firms rules and conditions

Continued access usually depends on maintaining acceptable trading performance and following the agreed risk limits

---

## Key Takeaway

Proprietary trading firms create opportunities for traders to access larger pools of trading capital without providing all of the capital themselves

Most firms require traders to complete an evaluation before granting funded status
