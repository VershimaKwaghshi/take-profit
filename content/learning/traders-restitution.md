# traders restitution

## What is restitution

Restitution is the process of getting back capital that was lost in the market without making another deposit.

Instead of asking you to add new money after a major loss, Take Profit works with its partner brokers to restore eligible trading capital when the platform rules have been met.

![Traders Restitution](/learning/traders-restitution.svg)

---

## Why restitution exists

Many traders do not stop because they lose skill.

They stop because they run out of capital.

Once an account is heavily reduced, the trader often has only two choices.

Deposit more money or stop trading.

Restitution was created to offer another path.

---

## How restitution works

If an eligible trading account reaches a fifty percent loss, the account becomes eligible for restitution.

When the platform rules have been met, Take Profit pays the partner broker to restore the lost capital.

The trader does not make another deposit for that restitution.

The goal is to help traders continue their journey instead of starting over.

---

## Why platform rules matter

Restitution is designed for active participants who continue to follow the platform rules.

The detailed requirements are explained in the Terms of Service and other platform guides.

---

## Key takeaway

Restitution is designed to reduce the need for repeated deposits after major losses.

By restoring eligible lost capital, traders can continue building experience without restarting from zero every time a significant setback occurs.
