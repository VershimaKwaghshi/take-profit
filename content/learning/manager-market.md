# Manager Market

## What Is The Manager Market

Not every trader has the time or experience to actively manage a trading account.

At the same time, many traders have the skill to manage more than one account.

The Manager Market brings both together.

It allows trading accounts to be managed across the platform, creating more opportunities for traders while every account remains owned by its original owner.

![Manager Market](/learning/manager-market.svg)

---

## How It Works

Once your trading account becomes active, Take Profit gradually assigns up to five additional trading accounts of the same size for you to manage.

Each assigned account belongs to another trader on the platform.

Your own trading account is also assigned to another trader for management.

Ownership never changes.

Only the management of the account changes, allowing traders to earn from multiple trading accounts at the same time.

---

## Becoming A Manager

Any trader can apply to become a manager.

To qualify, you must demonstrate consistent trading ability.

This can be achieved by submitting at least one month of verified live trading history or by successfully completing the Take Profit evaluation.

Once approved, every assigned account follows the standard two way profit sharing structure between the account owner and the manager.

---

## If You Are Not A Manager

You do not need to become a manager to benefit from the Manager Market.

Your trading account can still be managed by an approved manager while you continue earning from its performance.

Assigned accounts are also available to you.

Those accounts follow the standard three way profit sharing structure.

If you decide to trade an assigned account yourself without becoming a registered manager, the platform simply retains the manager allocation.

---

## Responsibility For Trading Losses

Trading always carries risk, and losses are part of trading.

Managers are responsible for making trading decisions, not replacing trading losses.

Account owners are also not required to repay trading losses.

Take Profit manages loss recovery through its own internal systems.

This allows traders and managers to focus on trading without becoming personally liable for trading losses.

---

## Why The Manager Market Exists

Some traders have more trading experience than time to build larger capital.

Others have capital but prefer experienced traders to manage it.

The Manager Market brings both together.

It allows trading knowledge to be shared across multiple accounts while ownership always remains with the original trader.

This creates more opportunities for everyone involved.

---

## Multiple Sources Of Income

Most traders normally earn from a single trading account.

The Manager Market allows several trading accounts to generate earnings at the same time.

Each account operates independently.

As more assigned accounts become active, traders have additional opportunities to earn without giving up ownership of their own trading account.

---

## Key Takeaway

The Manager Market allows traders to participate in multiple trading accounts while ownership always remains with the original account owner.

Experienced traders can manage additional accounts, while every trader can benefit from professional account management and multiple income opportunities.

Trading losses are managed through the Take Profit system, allowing traders and managers to focus on trading.
